Quick chat

Pick a set and change any of its phrases. Only you see your phrases in your game, and they show the next time you start the game. Leave a box empty to keep the original phrase. No commas, letters and numbers only, short, and nothing that is not allowed in chat.

Do not disturb

Turn this on for a while and only your favourites can invite you into a room, and you stop getting "friend is online" notices. It switches itself off when the time is up. Favourite a friend from your friends list to let them through.
